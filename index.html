Privacy Policy for Flappy Bat Night Edition

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Flappy Bat Night Edition (the "app"), a single-player arcade game developed by Vagner Nerves, handles information.

Information the app collects

The app does not collect, request, or transmit personal information. It does not require an account and has no online gameplay, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases. Game state and visual effects are processed temporarily on your device and are not sent to the developer. The app does not use the microphone; its thunder sound is a bundled audio file played by the app.

Storage and retention

The app does not maintain a developer-operated account or database and does not retain personal information. Temporary game state remains on your device while you play.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, share, or disclose information collected by the app because the app does not collect personal information. Google Play and Android may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies when you download or use apps through their services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe information has been provided to the developer, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's information practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL with its updated date.
